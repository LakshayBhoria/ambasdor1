Questions

When does a referral count?

When the student you referred has registered with your code and their payment is confirmed. Until then it shows as pending on your dashboard.

How do I get paid?

Commission is added to your balance for each paid referral. The ThinKorix team pays out your balance and records every payout on your dashboard.

What do I get at 20, 30 and 50 referrals?

At 20 you get a T-shirt and a cash bonus. At 30 you get a goodies pack, more gifts and a cash bonus. At 50 you get the full package: premium hamper, cash reward, a paid internship at ThinKorix with a stipend of up to ₹9,000+, offer letter, letter of recommendation, certificate and more gifts.

How do I claim a reward?

Once you reach a milestone, open your dashboard, choose Claim this reward and enter your delivery details. You can follow the status there.

Can I refer myself?

No. Referrals must be other students, and each student counts for one ambassador only.
